Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Applications Covered: SyncTrack Visitor Management System & SyncTrack Permit to Work Monitoring

1. Introduction

SyncTrack Visitor Management System and SyncTrack Permit to Work Monitoring ("we," "our," or "us") are committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our B2B SaaS applications handle data across mobile and web interfaces.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Subscription Data: Google Play billing tokens and order IDs used exclusively to provision your dedicated secure tenant_id.
  • Device Metadata: Terminal station names and 4-digit PINs used to authenticate up to 6 devices per tenant fleet.
  • Operational Data: Visitor logs, POB roster counts, permits, and isolation records.

3. How We Use Data

Data is strictly scoped by tenant_id for multi-tenant isolation, real-time WebSocket synchronization across terminals, and offline maritime licensing validation.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent data. Data is processed securely via Google Play Billing services and encrypted cloud endpoints. All records are isolated per organization.
